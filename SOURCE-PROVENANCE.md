# Source provenance

The hardware/controller procedure follows wilbowes/EchoMuse and bboe/firebreak.
Home Assistant, Wyoming faster-whisper, openWakeWord, Piper, Orpheus/SNAC,
Unsloth, vLLM, Home-LLM and Music Assistant are independent upstream projects.
The included controller compose originated upstream; its URLs identify it.
The local adapter, training/QA helpers, resource guards and integration examples
were developed as part of the reproducible workflow assembled here.
