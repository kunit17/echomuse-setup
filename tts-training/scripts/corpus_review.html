What to listen for
  • Every spoken word matches the transcript, without invented repeats.
  • The first and last words are complete. Listen for natural pauses.
  • No clicks, distortion, background speech or distracting empty sections.
  • The performed voice fits the voice you want to train.

A speaker flag signals variation, not a different person's identity. Correct only what was spoken; retain names and preserve the audio.
